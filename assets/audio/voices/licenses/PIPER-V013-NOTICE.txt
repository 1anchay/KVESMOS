KVESMOS French synthetic speech, November 2026

Original KVESMOS educational text synthesized locally using piper-tts 1.8.0,
fr_FR-siwis-medium, speaker 0, 22050 Hz; AAC encoding and loudness adjustment.
These are synthetic recordings, not recordings by a KVESMOS voice actor.
The synthesis engine and model weights are not distributed in this app.

Model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/fr/fr_FR/siwis/medium
Model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/fr/fr_FR/siwis/medium/MODEL_CARD
Dataset: The SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database, 2016.
Junichi Yamagishi, Pierre-Edouard Honnet, Philip Garner, Alexandros Lazaridis (2017).
University of Edinburgh, Centre for Speech Technology Research.
https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/1705

normalization, trimming by the engine and AAC compression.
