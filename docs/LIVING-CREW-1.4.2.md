# Living Characters 1.4.2

The eight neutral portraits use the approved artwork. The export script removes the opaque matte from seven of them for display in both themes. For Miko, Zig Quartz, Reyvo, Aster, Vespa, Nox and the fox professor, the new pose and expression sprites use separately selected frames from the approved character sheets. The export script records the cell coordinates, removes the concept sheet matte and exports compact transparent WebP. Original artwork and Orbi assets stay in place.

Pixie's sprite strip was created with built-in ImageGen using the approved Pixie artwork as the identity reference. Prompt: three equally sized transparent full-body sprites of the same cyan and pink faceted character, preserving the star eyes, antennae and purple gold-trimmed tool bag. Draw a thoughtful hand-to-cheek pose, a surprised pose holding a small gold and cobalt chest, and a joyous arms-raised celebration with crescent eyes and a gold star. No labels, backdrop or overlap.

The renderer selects actual drawings by context and performs finite pose transitions. Reduced motion and the visual reactions setting show the final illustration immediately. Speaking uses the existing real playback signal. The rendering is a short sprite performance rather than phoneme-synchronised animation.

The Android demo renders the production CharacterMoment widget for every hero in light and dark themes, then opens the real profile and changes the saved ThemeMode.
